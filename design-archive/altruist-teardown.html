Steal this, exactly

Their tint/ink pairs share one lightness with the hue rotated. Drop these into @theme in src/index.css — they're already in the same blue family as your #1F4C63.

Blue#deeff8 → #16526e
Peach#fff2e3 → #6e4616
Citron#f9fae3 → #6e5f16
Orchid#fadbff → #6e166a
Deep#16526e → #deeff8
/* src/index.css — replace the token block */
@theme {
  /* ground: warm, never pure white */
  --color-paper:      #FFFFFC;
  --color-paper-tint: #EBF8FF;   /* alternate every 2nd section */
  --color-paper-deep: #16526E;   /* one dark band per page */

  /* ink: opacity ladder, not four greys */
  --color-ink:      #0A0A0A;
  --color-ink-60:   rgba(0,0,0,.6);  /* all body copy */
  --color-ink-40:   rgba(0,0,0,.4);  /* captions only */

  /* ONE accent. delete amber, gold, primary-*, blue-50..900 */
  --color-accent:      #16526E;
  --color-accent-lift: #116E99;  /* eyebrows, highlighted words */

  /* tint → ink pairs (same L, hue rotated) */
  --color-tint-blue:   #DEEFF8;  --color-on-blue:   #16526E;
  --color-tint-peach:  #FFF2E3;  --color-on-peach:  #6E4616;
  --color-tint-citron: #F9FAE3;  --color-on-citron: #6E5F16;

  /* radius scales with the element */
  --radius-btn:   10px;
  --radius-card:  17px;
  --radius-panel: 26px;
  --radius-pill:  999px;  /* badges only, not CTAs */

  /* depth: the inset is what you're missing */
  --shadow-card:  0 1px 10px rgba(0,0,0,.06);
  --shadow-lit:   inset 0 0 14px rgba(22,82,110,.08);
  --shadow-lift:  0 3px 6px rgba(0,0,0,.15);
}

Do these, in this order

Ranked by aesthetic gain per hour. The first three are the ones anyone would notice.

01

Put photographs on the page highest impact

2 images across 4253px is the reason it feels like documentation. Altruist runs 38, starting with a 1245×664 full-bleed hero. You need real faces: a wide photo behind the hero (you already have the dark overlay), one photo per step in "Three steps", and a face beside each testimonial. Nothing else on this list moves the needle as far.

02

Kill the 14px body copy highest impact

Your step cards, feature copy and most descriptions run 14px/400. Theirs run 20px/500 at 60% black. Go 17px minimum for card copy, 20px for section intros, and switch from #2D4A5A to rgba(0,0,0,.6) — an opacity ladder always harmonises with whatever ground it sits on; a fixed grey doesn't.

03

Block the page in colour highest impact

Every one of your five sections is transparent on white with identical 144px padding. Alternate #FFFFFC → #EBF8FF → #FFFFFC, and put "Why volunteering matters" on the full #16526E dark band with white type. You already defined --color-paper-2 and --color-paper-3 and never used them — the system exists, it's just unwired.

04

Commit or delete Fraunces and Outfit quick win

The live page downloads Fraunces (4 weights, variable optical size) and Outfit (5 weights) and renders them on 6 elements total out of 272. Either give Fraunces a real job — every H2 on the page, or every stat number — or cut both imports. Right now it's pure payload with no visual return, and it contradicts your own --font-sans: Outfit token.

05

Headings to 700 at -0.04em quick win

Your H1 is 56px/600/-0.025em; theirs is 54px/700/-0.04em. Smaller and it hits harder, because weight and tracking do the work instead of size. One-line change, disproportionate effect.

06

Un-pill the CTAs quick win

rounded-full + 14px/500 labels is the default every AI-generated landing page ships. Move primary buttons to 10px radius, 15px 20px padding, and a 16px/600 label. Keep 9999px for badges and chips only — that contrast is itself a design signal.

07

Add inset light to tinted surfaces polish

All five of your shadows are grey drop shadows. Their tinted cards carry inset 0 0 14px rgba(22,82,110,.08), which makes a flat pastel look lit from within rather than painted on. Add it to every card that sits on a tint.

08

Cut three of your four accent families polish

src/index.css ships teal #1F4C63, amber #E08A3C, gold #B98A22, a green primary-* ramp, plus full legacy blue-50…900 and orange-50…900 scales. Altruist runs one blue and treats pastels as decoration that never carries meaning. Pick the teal, delete the rest, replace their uses with the tint pairs above.

09

Vary the vertical rhythm polish

144px on every section is metronomic. Give the hero 140px top, compress the logo/proof band to ~60px, and let the two heaviest sections run 160–180px. A page needs a pulse; identical spacing flatlines it.

The one-sentence version

Your design system is already good — it's unspent. The paper layers, the radius scale and the display font are all defined in src/index.css and almost none of it reaches the screen. Altruist isn't richer, it just cashes in everything it declares, then puts photographs behind it.
