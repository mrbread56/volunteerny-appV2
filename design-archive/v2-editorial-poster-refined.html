How it works

Three steps, and none of them involve paper forms.

01

Tell us what you care about

Your school, your grade, the causes you want to work on, and when you are actually free. Takes about two minutes.

02

Apply to real placements

Every organisation on here is verified before students can see it. Apply in one tap and track where each application stands.

03

Your supervisor signs off

They confirm your hours in the app. Your total updates the moment they do, and you export the record your school needs.
